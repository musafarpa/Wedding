# Wedding

A romantic 3D wedding invitation web experience for **Ananya & Rohan** celebrating their union in Kumarakom, Kerala.

## Features
- **Cinematic Hero**: Chibi bride & groom wedding animation film with ambient Kerala theme
- **3D Invitation Card Splash Screen**: Interactive "Tap to Open" 3D envelope with royal monogram wax seal, book unfold, and floating petals
- **The Romantic Story**: Pinned GSAP ScrollTrigger timeline with nilavilakku progress track and archival temple arch photos
- **Background Music**: Romantic audio track ("Love At First Sight")
- **Interactive Wedding Details**: Schedule of ceremonies (Haldi, Mehendi, Muhurtham, Reception), venue maps, and RSVP countdown
